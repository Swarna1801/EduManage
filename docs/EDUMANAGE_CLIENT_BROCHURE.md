# EduManage Client Brochure

## Tagline

Run your school with more clarity, less manual work, and better parent engagement.

## What Is EduManage?

EduManage is a smart school management platform that connects principals, teachers, parents, and super admins in one unified system. It helps schools manage academic operations, communication, onboarding, fees, and reporting without relying on fragmented tools.

## Why Schools Choose EduManage

Schools often struggle with disconnected records, delayed communication, manual attendance work, scattered marksheets, and inefficient onboarding. EduManage brings these workflows together so schools can operate faster and make better decisions with less effort.

## Who It Is For

### Principals

- Get a complete school dashboard
- Monitor teachers, attendance, marks, and fees
- Send announcements and notifications
- Review school-level analytics

### Teachers

- Mark attendance quickly
- Upload marks and exam records
- Access assigned students and class data
- Manage timetable and leave-related workflows
- Use parent onboarding links in premium plans

### Parents

- Track attendance and academic progress
- Check fee status and updates
- View timetable and notifications
- Stay connected with school communication

### Platform Owners

- Manage multiple schools from one place
- Control school subscriptions and plan upgrades
- Send priority platform-level updates

## Key Benefits

- One platform for attendance, marks, fees, notifications, timetable, and communication
- Role-specific dashboards for every stakeholder
- Faster onboarding with premium invite-link workflows
- Better visibility for principals and better transparency for parents
- Scalable plan structure for schools of different sizes

## Basic vs Premium

### Basic Plan

- Suitable for schools that need essential digital operations
- Supports attendance, marks, fees, notifications, and timetable workflows
- Student limit up to 500

### Premium Plan

- Designed for schools that want deeper engagement and advanced visibility
- Adds advanced analytics, chat, part-wise marks, and self-onboarding invite links
- Student limit up to 1500

## How EduManage Helps In Daily School Operations

- Teachers manage classroom activity from a structured dashboard
- Principals review school performance from one central view
- Parents receive timely updates instead of waiting for offline communication
- Super admins manage subscription and school lifecycle centrally

## Positioning Statement

EduManage is more than a school ERP. It is a school operating system built to connect administration, academics, parent communication, and school growth in one practical digital workflow.

## Suggested Sales Pitch

EduManage helps schools move from manual coordination to connected operations. With separate dashboards for principals, teachers, parents, and platform admins, the platform makes it easier to manage attendance, marks, fees, communication, and onboarding in one place. Schools can start with core workflows on the Basic plan and expand into advanced analytics, chat, and invite-based onboarding through Premium.

## Ideal Use Cases

- New schools digitizing operations
- Growing schools replacing spreadsheets and WhatsApp-heavy workflows
- Schools that want stronger principal oversight
- Schools that need better parent visibility and communication
- Multi-school operators managing subscriptions centrally

## Closing Message

EduManage helps schools save time, improve visibility, strengthen communication, and scale operations with confidence.
